About SentiMind

SentiMind was designed to offer a calm, non-judgmental space for students to reflect on their digital routines. In a hyper-connected world, digital fatigue and screen routines frequently influence our internal focus and well-being.

To precisely calculate your digital well-being score, SentiMind uses a **Random Forest Regressor** machine learning model. This model evaluates 12 key features covering your demographics (age, gender, academic level, and country), digital routine (most used platform, primary purpose, daily usage hours, and device unlocks), and lifestyle habits (study, sleep, exercise hours, and self-perceived stress level).

The model functions by aggregating predictions from an ensemble of decision trees, capturing complex and non-linear interactions between screen time, sleep quality, and physical activity. By parsing these inputs through standard preprocessing transformers and applying regression analysis, the algorithm projects a balance score out of 10 that reflects your overall routine alignment with healthy well-being profiles.

Your Data Privacy

Your privacy is absolute. SentiMind operates under an empathy-first data protocol:

  • No Local Logs: None of the values you select in the sliders or radio groups are logged, stored, or linked to your browser profile.
  • Session-Only: Inputs exist purely inside temporary browser variables to make the prediction request, and disappear the moment you refresh or close the page.
  • Local Server: The prediction calculations run locally on your own system. No third-party servers ever read your inputs.
